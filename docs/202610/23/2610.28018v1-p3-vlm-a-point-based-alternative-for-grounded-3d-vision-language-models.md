## 摘要
审视现有用于 3D VLM 定位（grounding）的基准测试，可以发现评估与提示协议中普遍存在一种“语义泄漏”：严重依赖边界框作为空间提示或表示基元。由于边界框对物体类别具有高度信息量，它们为模型引入了一条捷径，使其在 3D VLM 训练期间忽视视觉信息。我们用一个“盲”LLM 证明了这一点：仅向其提供框坐标作为提示，它就能取得出奇好的表现。为进一步分析这一捷径，我们提出基于点的 grounding 提示——用单个 3D 点而非边界框来查询模型。在该协议下，盲 LLM 在 ScanNet 的 Nr3D 上的准确率从 36.4% 降至 18.0%，3D VLM（如 LL3DA）也出现类似下降。基于这一洞见，我们提出 P$^3$-VLM，一种新颖的三通路 3D VLM，支持基于点的提示，并在该设置下于位置接地的描述生成任务上取得 52.1% 的最先进准确率。为实现这一点，我们重新设计了架构，同时保持其无检测器、以场景为中心，并适用于 3D GS 输入。P$^3$-VLM 利用三条互补通路（位置感知、任务感知和上下文感知），从而在完全不依赖边界框的情况下实现对场景的强推理。基于点的学习的关键优势在于跨设置的一致提升：P$^3$-VLM 在基于点的 grounding 下达到最先进性能，同时提升了鲁棒性、OOD 泛化能力，以及在非 grounding、以场景为中心的 3D VQA 基准上的性能，这反映出其减少了对边界框捷径的依赖，并由此获得更强的视觉-空间推理能力。

## Abstract
Examining existing benchmarks for grounding in 3D VLMs reveals a pervasive "semantic leakage" in the evaluation and prompting protocols: a heavy reliance on bounding boxes as spatial prompts or representation primitives. Because bounding boxes are highly informative of object categories, they introduce a shortcut for the models to neglect visual information during 3D VLM training. We demonstrate this with a "blind" LLM that, prompted only with the box coordinates, can perform surprisingly well. To further analyze this shortcut, we propose point-based grounding prompting - querying models with a single 3D point instead of a box. Under this protocol, the accuracy of the blind LLM drops from 36.4% to 18.0% on ScanNet's Nr3D, and similarly degrades for 3D VLMs (e.g. LL3DA).   Building on this insight, we introduce P$^3$-VLM, a novel tri-pathway 3D VLM that supports point-based prompts and achieves 52.1% state-of-the-art accuracy on location-grounded captioning under this setting. To achieve this, we redesign the architecture while keeping it detector-free, scene-centric, and suitable for 3D GS inputs. P$^3$-VLM utilizes three complementary pathways (location-, task-, and context-aware), enabling strong reasoning about scenes without any bounding box dependencies. The key benefit of point-based learning is a consistent improvement across settings: P$^3$-VLM achieves state-of-the-art performance under point-based grounding while also improving robustness, OOD generalization, and performance on ungrounded scene-centric 3D VQA benchmarks, reflecting its reduced reliance on bounding-box shortcuts and resulting stronger visual-spatial reasoning.
