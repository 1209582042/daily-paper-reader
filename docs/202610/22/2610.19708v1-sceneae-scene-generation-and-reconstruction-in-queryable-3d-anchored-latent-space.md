tldr: 视频世界模型常用3D记忆整合历史观测，但能否直接把3D表示作为场景重建与生成的潜空间仍待探索。SceneAE将场景表示为稀疏的3D锚定潜点云，每个潜变量由显式3D位置与隐式内容特征构成，直接描述场景本身而非特定视角外观。重建时可由不同目标相机投影解码为RGB-D观测，无需逐条轨迹重生成视频；生成时先相机控制生成3D几何再合成内容，降低相机与外观纠缠。实验显示其在生成质量、计算成本和相机可控性上更优。

## 摘要
近期的视频世界模型越来越多地采用三维记忆机制，将历史观测整合为轻量且持久的场景表示。这自然引出一个互补的问题：如果三维表示能够作为记忆已观测场景的有效机制，那么它们是否也能直接用作重建和生成场景的隐空间？受此启发，我们将场景表示为一个紧凑且无结构的三维锚定隐空间（SceneAE）。每个隐变量将显式三维位置与隐式内容特征相结合，并在空间上定位以描述其局部场景区域，从而形成稀疏的潜空间点云。与描述场景在特定视角下如何呈现的传统视频VAE隐变量不同，我们的表示直接描述场景本身，因此自然适用于重建和生成。对于重建，一旦场景生成完成，我们持久的三维表示可以由不同目标相机投影并解码为RGB-D观测，而无需为每条新的相机轨迹重新生成视频。对于生成，我们利用几何与内容的分离，首先生成相机可控的三维几何，然后以其为条件合成内容，从而减少相机与外观的纠缠并提升相机可控性。实验表明，该三维隐空间在生成质量、计算开销和相机可控性方面均取得更优表现。

## Abstract
Recent video world models have increasingly adopted 3D memory mechanisms to integrate historical observations into a lightweight and persistent scene representation. This naturally raises a complementary question: if 3D representations can serve as an effective mechanism for memorizing observed scenes, can they also be directly used as the latent space for reconstructing and generating scenes? Motivated by this idea, we represent a scene as a compact and unstructured 3D-anchored latent space (\textbf{SceneAE}). Each latent combines an   explicit 3D position with an implicit content feature and is spatially grounded to describe its local scene region, forming a   sparse latent point cloud. Unlike conventional video-VAE latents that describe how a scene appears from particular viewpoints, our   representation directly describes the scene itself, making it naturally applicable to both reconstruction and generation. For   reconstruction, once the scene has been generated, our persistent 3D representation can be projected and decoded into RGB-D observations by different target cameras, without the need to regenerate a video for each new camera trajectory.   For generation, we leverage the geometry-content separation to first generate camera-controlled 3D geometry and   then synthesize content conditioned on it, reducing camera-appearance entanglement and improving camera   controllability. Experiments show that this 3D latent achieves superior generation quality, computational cost, and camera controllability.
